## additiveHelix

Add material by sweeping a closed, Body-owned cross-section around an axis while advancing axially, optionally with radial growth. Choose it for a coil or a helical boss: start an empty Body or continue its current solid tip. Profile offset sets the radius; pitch and section size must leave one valid solid. This typed family is under qualification; a passing short coil does not qualify every long-coil or growth case.

Common mistakes and assumptions:

- Supply exactly the independent dimensions for the selected mode; a derived dimension cannot also be authored or driven.
- Self-intersection, invalid native solids, unresolved precision or exhausted resource limits refuse the request.

Native binding: `PartDesign::AdditiveHelix`. Output kind: `["solid"]`.

| `mode` | Choose which independent dimensions describe the helix: pitchHeight needs pitch and height; pitchTurns needs pitch and turns; heightTurns needs height and turns; heightTurnsGrowth needs height, turns and radial growth. ## additiveLoft

Add material through an ordered list of at least two closed Body-owned section profiles. It may start the Body or join the current base. ruled gives straight ruled spans; otherwise the native loft interpolates smoothly. Sections retain their placements/attachments and the result must be one valid positive-volume solid.

## body

A native container for one manufactured part and its ordered editable history. Start an independent Body for another part; profiles and datums belong to exactly one Body. The first additive feature has no base and every later feature explicitly continues this Body’s current solid tip. Multiple Bodies share one document, but output publishes only one final solid tip. Bodies are containers, not exported solids.

## bodySeed

Start an editable PartDesign Body from an earlier checked solid, retaining the source link and original geometry.
