## plate

Choose plate for a rectangular mounting blank with positioned through-holes. Width/depth define the XY outline, height sets thickness, and optional holes remove material through both faces. Produces checked STL, generated SCAD and a geometry summary.

Native binding: `difference / cube / translated cylinder through-holes`. Output kind: `["geometry","text"]`.

## spacer

Choose spacer for a concentric sleeve or washer. Supply outer diameter, bore diameter and height in mm; it is centred on XY origin and extends upwards from Z=0. Polygonal walls approximate circles, so across-flats bore clearance differs from nominal diameter.
