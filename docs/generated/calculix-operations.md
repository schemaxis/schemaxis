## cantileverBeam

Analyses a straight rectangular cantilever in linear static elasticity: the x = 0 end is fully clamped and a vertical (z) force acts on the free end. Schemaxis generates CalculiX C3D20R quadratic solid meshes at three resolutions, solves them, and checks tip deflection against Euler-Bernoulli theory, support reactions against the load, and interior bending stress. It publishes the fine editable deck beam.inp, interior-stress.csv (Gauss-point stresses in Pa for 0.3 <= x/L <= 0.7) and beam-assessment.json. Choose it for quick stiffness/serviceability checks of slender beams; it is not a general FEA tool.

Common mistakes and assumptions:

- Geometry must be slender (length / larger section side 20 to 40, width/height 0.5 to 2) and the predicted tip deflection at most 1% of the length; otherwise planning fails with INVALID_BEAM.
- Only linear elasticity: no yield, plasticity, buckling, contact, dynamics or temperature effects are modelled.
- The stress output excludes the clamp and the loaded end, so it is not the peak stress of the beam; do not use it for yield or fatigue checks.
- A correctly solved beam can still fail its declared requirements (PERFORMANCE_NOT_MET); its results are retained but not published to later steps.

Native binding: `CalculiX C3D20R linear static`. Output kind: `["table","text"]`.

Inputs: none.
