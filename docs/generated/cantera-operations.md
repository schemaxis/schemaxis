## equilibrate

For each listed initial temperature, sets the gas to that temperature and its pressure and then calls Cantera's ThermoPhase.equilibrate with the element-potential solver, holding either temperature and pressure (TP) or specific enthalpy and pressure (HP, adiabatic). It publishes the same results.csv columns as thermoSweep, now with the equilibrium composition and, for HP, the adiabatic final temperature, plus chemistry-summary.json with element-conservation and chemical-potential residuals. Choose it for dissociation at fixed temperature or adiabatic flame temperatures of hydrogen mixtures; choose thermoSweep for a non-reacting property table.

Common mistakes and assumptions:

- Rows are independent starting states, not a transient trajectory; equilibrium says nothing about how fast the state is reached.
- In HP mode the final temperature must also lie within 300-3500 K; an undiluted hot mixture can exceed it and fail verification, so add N2 or AR or lower the initial temperature.
- Independent verification checks element inventories, NASA7 properties and that every accessible species' chemical potential matches the element potentials within 2e-5 RT.

Native binding: `cantera.ThermoPhase.equilibrate`. Output kind: `["table"]`.

## gas

Defines the starting gas state for the table node: a pressure in pascals and a hydrogen/oxygen/inert mixture drawn from the ten species of Cantera's h2o2.yaml ideal-gas phase ohmech. It produces no table itself; it is always the first of the two nodes, and the thermoSweep or equilibrate node that follows evaluates it at each requested temperature. Amounts are proportions on the chosen mole or mass basis and are normalised, so they need not sum to one. Choose another tool when the mixture contains carbon, NOx or condensed phases; this mechanism cannot represent them.

Common mistakes and assumptions:

- Each species may appear only once and the amounts must have a positive total, otherwise planning fails with INVALID_COMPOSITION.
- N2 and AR are inert in this mechanism: no nitrogen oxides or argon compounds ever form, even at high temperature.
- The gas is ideal; no real-gas correction is applied, so states near condensation or at the upper pressure limit are idealised.

Native binding: `cantera.Solution`. Output kind: `["gas"]`.

Inputs: none.

## thermoSweep

Evaluates the gas at each listed temperature with its composition and pressure frozen, publishing results.csv (one row per temperature: density, specific enthalpy, internal energy, entropy, cp, cv, mean molecular weight and the unchanged mole and mass fractions) plus chemistry-summary.json. Choose it for property tables of a non-reacting mixture, for example heat capacity of a diluent or density of a fixed fuel/air mixture; choose equilibrate when the composition should be allowed to react to chemical equilibrium. The host recomputes every property from the public NASA7 coefficients and a second Cantera process reloads the saved states before the run is accepted.

Common mistakes and assumptions:

- No reaction occurs: a hydrogen/oxygen mixture listed at 3000 K is reported unreacted, which is physically unstable; use equilibrate for reacting states.
- Rows are independent states, not a heating process; their order carries no history.
- Keep chemistry-summary.json with the CSV; the CSV alone does not record the mechanism, basis or units.

Native binding: `cantera.ThermoPhase`. Output kind: `["table"]`.
