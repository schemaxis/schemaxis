## linearRegression

Fits an ordinary least-squares linear model (response = intercept + sum of coefficient x predictor) to 1 to 8 numeric predictors with R's stats::lm, then reopens the saved model in a fresh R process and checks every coefficient, prediction and residual against an independent calculation. It publishes coefficients.csv, training.csv, heldout.csv (when held-out rows are given) and regression-summary.json. Choose it for calibration curves or simple explanatory fits whose error limits you can state in advance.

Common mistakes and assumptions:

- There must be more training rows than coefficients, and the predictors must not be (nearly) linearly dependent; otherwise RANK_DEFICIENT_DATA or ILL_CONDITIONED_DATA.
- Only additive numeric terms: no categorical variables, interactions, polynomials or custom formulas.
- No confidence intervals, hypothesis tests or causal claims; held-out errors describe only the rows you supplied.
- A correct fit that misses a declared requirement fails with FIT_REQUIREMENT_NOT_MET and is not passed to later workflow steps.

Native binding: `stats::lm / base::saveRDS / stats::predict`. Output kind: `["table","text"]`.

Inputs: none.
