## principalComponents

Find the dominant correlated patterns in captured multi-channel measurements. The route centres each channel at its mean, scales it as declared, and computes principal components by singular value decomposition in isolated NumPy. It publishes the spectrum with explained variance, labelled loadings and scores (or a retained-subspace projector) and a reconstruction in the channels' own units, each independently checked by a standard-library verifier.

Common mistakes and assumptions:

- Repeated or near-repeated eigenvalues never publish an arbitrary per-component basis; use subspace output.
- Accuracy is relative to the total variance, not to each small eigenvalue; no statistical significance or sampling-uncertainty claim is made.
- Requirements are decided by the host verifier; unmet or indeterminate results are retained evidence, not accepted deliverables.

Native binding: `numpy.linalg.svd(full_matrices=False) on the centred, scaled data (LAPACK gesdd)`. Output kind: `["table","text"]`.

Inputs: none.
