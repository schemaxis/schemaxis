# FFmpeg `extractAudio`: AAC in MP4/M4A to a verified PCM16 WAV — 25 October 2026

Phone and camera recordings are usually AAC in MP4 or M4A. The PCM16 WAV and FLAC
bridges (SoX, FLAC, whisper.cpp) could not take them. This allocation adds one
operation, `extractAudio`, to the existing FFmpeg adaptor (FFmpeg 9.0.2 already ships
a native AAC decoder) instead of a new adaptor. It is a platform-gap judgement, not
evidence of customer demand. Existing operations, their encoding recipes, `fresh.py`,
`review.py` and `video.py` are unchanged.

## Product and bounds

- Authored node: `filter: extractAudio`, `options: {file, streamIndex, threads}`.
  `file` is an absolute `.mp4`/`.m4a` path or an earlier file artifact; `streamIndex`
  (0 to 15, global, required) is never inferred.
- Accepted: one AAC-LC (`mp4a`) stream, mono or stereo, at an AAC rate from 8000 to
  96000 Hz, plus optionally one H.264 video stream; intact, non-fragmented,
  self-contained MP4 (brands isom, iso2, mp41, mp42, M4A, M4V); at most 64 MiB of
  compressed input, 600 decoded seconds and a 32 MiB canonical WAV (44-byte header).
- Refused (typed faults, nothing partial published): extra audio, subtitle, data and
  cover-art streams, chapters and programs, other codecs and profiles (HE-AAC),
  surround, a stream not starting at zero or not in a 1/rate time base, gaps or
  overlaps in the decoded audio frames, fragmented or externally referenced MP4,
  truncated or damaged files, and anything over the limits.
- Output: `audio.wav` (host-built canonical header, native PCM16 at the source rate and
  channel count, no resampling, mixing, trimming or padding) and `audio-summary.json`
  (captured codec, stream index, decoded geometry and duration, PCM digest,
  `status: machineDraft`, loss labels).
- AAC is lossy. No equality with the recording that was encoded, no recognition
  accuracy and no human review is claimed. The captured file is an unauthenticated
  trust anchor.

## Native facts that decided the design

FFmpeg 9.0.2, explicit `-f mp4` encodes of generated PCM (`probe-aac-semantics.py`,
every process recorded with PID, timeout and exit code):

- Decoded PCM frames equal the sum of the decoded audio-frame `nb_samples` that ffprobe
  lists and the stream `duration_ts`, for mono 8/16/22.05/96 kHz, stereo 32/44.1/48 kHz,
  a 1-frame clip and H.264 + AAC files. Frame timestamps are contiguous from zero in the
  1/rate time base and the last frame is partial.
- `nb_frames` (packets) is not the audio-frame count: the priming packet is trimmed by
  the edit list (for 12345 frames: 14 packets, 13 decoded frames, 12345 samples).
  `nb_frames * 1024` and the container duration are therefore not used. The host
  validates the bounded decoded-frame listing (contiguity, count cap, rate, duration and
  WAV-size caps) before decoding, then requires the native PCM frame count to equal it.
- The minimal build has only the `wav`, `mp4` and `mov` muxers, so the native decode is
  written as WAV (`-fflags +bitexact`, no LIST chunk; fmt extensible above 48 kHz) and the
  host parses it strictly and builds its own canonical 44-byte WAV. `-fs`/`-t` are not
  used; the kernel file-size limit and the exact host count catch truncation.

## Trust and verification

- The trusted FFmpeg and sibling ffprobe come from an explicit executable, stored
  configuration or `FFMPEG_EXECUTABLE` for both `run` and `verify`; never `PATH`, a
  receipt, a submission or authored input. No FFmpeg operation uses a `PATH` fallback.
- `run` captures the source once, checks the MP4 boxes host-side (no native process),
  then runs the fixed stages `source-probe`, `source-frames` and `decode` through
  adaptor-owned launchers: forced `mov` demuxer, `file` protocol only, `enable_drefs 0`,
  `use_absolute_path 0`, POSIX address-space, CPU, file-size and core limits, the shared
  deadline and cancellation, each stage bound to the digest of the exact file it opens.
- `verify` (also at the end of `run`) copies the held capture to a private directory,
  repeats all stages with the trusted runtime, rebuilds the canonical WAV and requires it
  to equal the published `audio.wav` byte for byte, then recomputes the summary,
  receipt and result (closed, typed JSON; duplicate keys, `NaN`, wrong types refused).
  Retained launchers, bindings, exit codes and the retained native readback are
  consistency diagnostics. Faults from the fresh pass carry `observed.fresh: true`.
- Not guarded: a coherent rewrite of the capture and every retained file describes a
  different valid recording and verifies (tested and documented); a different FFmpeg
  build that rounds float-to-PCM16 differently fails rather than being tolerated.

## Review 30 repair (same allocation, 25 October 2026)

An independent review (A1 to A5) accepted the trust and full-PCM verification design and asked for fault
classification, probe budget and wording fixes. No feature was added. Changed: `audio.py`, `test_ffmpeg_audio.py`,
`tools/qualify_ffmpeg_audio.py` (limits text only), the ffmpeg `readiness.json`, `guidance.json`, `capabilities.py`,
`upstream.json` and `docs/ffmpeg.md`, and this note. `adaptor.py` (identical to the first-pass 48 patch), `fresh.py`,
`review.py` and `video.py` are untouched.

- **A1, runtime failures are not source faults.** `launch` caught every `RUNNER_FAILED` and turned it into
  `INVALID_RESOURCE`, and a nonzero native exit raised before `video.check_diagnostics` ran. Now a nonzero exit
  is classified from its retained stderr like a zero exit: source-reading diagnostics are `INVALID_RESOURCE`;
  loader, allocation and unclassified output stay `RUNNER_FAILED` (source validity was not established); empty
  stderr re-raises the original runner fault; a zero exit that wrote no output is `RUNNER_FAILED`. Timeouts,
  cancellation and unavailable-runtime faults keep their codes. While verifying, each stage fault becomes
  `VERIFICATION_FAILED` with the cause retained (the fresh pass still tags `observed.fresh`).
- **A2, probe budgets.** The probe kernel file-size cap was 8 MiB while the host reads 4 MiB, so a metadata tag
  that pushed the probe output past 4 MiB failed a run as `VERIFICATION_FAILED`, not `RESOURCE_LIMIT`. The cap is now
  `MAX_PROBE + 1` (output of exactly 4 MiB still completes; a file that reaches the cap is over budget), and a
  stage that reaches its cap or is killed by `SIGXFSZ` is `RESOURCE_LIMIT`, decided before any source diagnostic
  is read; a decode that reaches the counted-size cap is `RESOURCE_LIMIT` too. Nothing is truncated or accepted.
  Native check (FFmpeg 9.0.2): before the fix a 5 MiB and a 9 MiB metadata tag both failed a run as
  `VERIFICATION_FAILED` ("required bounded regular file is unavailable") at `source-probe.stdout`; after it both
  are `RESOURCE_LIMIT` and the probe output stops at exactly 4 MiB + 1 bytes. The launcher is a Python process, and
  Python ignores `SIGXFSZ` (inherited by the exec'd child), so a capped stage gets `EFBIG` and normally exits 0
  with truncated output (observed: exit code 0, 4194305 bytes); the file reaching the cap is therefore the primary
  signal and the `SIGXFSZ` exit status only a secondary one. `-show_entries` was not restricted.
- **A2, packet versus frame bound.** `-read_intervals %+#N` counts packets of the selected stream, and the edit
  list trims the priming packet to no frame (verified natively: N packets list N-1 frames). The frame listing is
  now complete only when the stream's container packet count (`nb_frames`) is within `MAX_PACKETS` (17600);
  more is `RESOURCE_LIMIT` naming the probe bound, not the source. The bound was raised from 16401 packets (which
  a mono 960-sample AAC-LC stream within 32 MiB, up to 17476 frames, could exceed) to cover that profile:
  `MAX_FRAMES` 17500 decoded frames, `MAX_PACKETS` 17600. A source with a very long edit-list lead-in can still
  reach the packet bound first; that refusal is documented. FFmpeg's native encoder produces only 1024-sample
  frames, so the 960-sample case is covered by arithmetic and a host test, not a native source. This is not a
  claim of exhaustive AAC-LC coverage. The decode-versus-counted size guard is unchanged and no `-fs`/`-t` was added.
- **A3.** The route `context` (purpose, assumptions, constraints, inspection, recovery) and the capability
  requirements now describe five operations: no audio except the three-stream `muxReview` and `extractAudio`;
  recovery separates `UNSUPPORTED_AUDIO` for PCM WAV in `muxReview` from the AAC-LC refusals of `extractAudio`,
  and covers `streamIndex`, `RESOURCE_LIMIT`, `RUNNER_FAILED` and the trusted runtime.
- **A4.** The `muxReview` verification wording said native decoding was not replayed offline although the same
  field described a fresh native decode. It now says host re-derivation plus a fresh native decode.
- **A5.** The audio-plus-video case compared a value with itself. The generated `av` clip is checked against a
  separate native decode parsed by `wave`, and the shipped `extract-audio-video.mp4` fixture is checked against
  the literal 24000 frames (sample rate 16000, mono).

Launcher and evidence effect: the probe launcher text (`RLIMIT_FSIZE`) and the `source-frames` argv

## Limits

Serial runs on one FFmpeg 9.0.2 build; no HE-AAC, surround, fragmented MP4, non-MP4
containers or other codecs; no exhaustive MP4 coverage; AAC lossy; machine draft; no
authenticated capture; no signed recording or encoder-history claim.
