## Limits and provenance

Numeric ASCII CSV only: at most1MiB,2048rows,12columns,4series; no missing data,
unit conversion, fitting, asymmetric/x uncertainty, arbitrary scripts/equations,
3D,multiplots,PDF/EPS,MPI,live native editing/reverse sync or time batches.
Uncertainty meaning and units are asserted rather than statistically inferred.
Fixed typography/margins and connected circular samples; no complete raster,
font, accessibility or colour-management equivalence. Overdraw can legitimately
fail independent raster identification. Numeric tick formatting must remain
faithful within the coordinate tolerance, and verification requires at least two
visible major tick labels per axis; highly compressed or extreme ranges can fail
rather than publish misleading axes. POSIX native resource limits are required.
