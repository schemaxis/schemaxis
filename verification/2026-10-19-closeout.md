# Bounded programme closeout — 19 October 2026

This development checkpoint preserves the core, Studio and adaptor work since
the first public preview. A local commit does not itself establish acceptance.

The programme has accepted twelve new adaptors and six strengthened adaptors.
Three new adaptors were accepted in the preceding architecture integration.
Premiere's strengthening has host verification only; native verification remains
unavailable in the tested environment.

At this checkpoint, the remaining technical gates are:

- Final review of the shared saved-run path correction.
- FreeCAD phase-three lifecycle, deadline and evidence-reading repairs, followed
  by independent review and final qualification. Phases one and two are accepted.
- Final integration and source-current qualification for NumPy and pyproj.

NumPy and pyproj are not yet accepted programme deliveries. Their programme credit
also depends on the outstanding strengthening under the two-new-to-one rule.
Multipart PrusaSlicer is parked at the prototype/design stage; it is not a public
implementation. The existing single-part PrusaSlicer adaptor remains separate.

The latest completed host baseline ran 1,962 tests: 1,929 passed and 33 skipped,
with no failures or errors. The Studio baseline ran 466 tests: 463 passed and
three skipped. Both are historical baselines for the remaining repairs, not
evidence that unfinished work is accepted. Generated reference, Help, guidance,
admission and public release checks passed on that baseline.

Earlier adaptor qualification records describe the versions and source snapshots
they tested. Shared changes can make their conservative source fingerprints
stale; they are not blanket claims of fresh native testing of every adaptor.

The broader programme is paused for bounded closeout. No further batches are
authorised by this checkpoint.
