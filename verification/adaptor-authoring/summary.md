## Limits

- Derived statuses state declaration or structural facts with their basis. They are not reviews, and an implemented derived status is not native qualification.
- Surface kinds (identity, reference, subset-candidate, path, map) and nested units (members, map entries, discriminated variants) are structural candidates found from schema shape and field names. A row says that a unit exists, not that it needs a Name, multi-mode or a subset control; all four dimensions stay unassessed until audited.
- Authoring surfaces record projection facts only: Studio Add from the catalogue, quick rows and Batch actions from a declared multi-mode policy. Edit, Properties, core, CLI, console and AI behaviour is unassessed per item; sharing a schema is not evidence of shared behaviour.
- Contract fingerprints cover each field's own validation keywords together with its guidance control, authoring and fileExtensions records, declarations, catalogue entries and operation guidance authoring records. Only schema annotations in keyword position (description, title, $comment, Studio guide annotations) and catalogue display prose are excluded; literal defaults, constants, enumerations, declarations and guidance control/authoring/fileExtensions records are verbatim. Field guidance drifts only through its status, control, authoring and fileExtensions; its explanation, label, units, defaultMeaning, pitfalls, examples, sources and question are documentation and do not drift.
- Admitted identity/reference policy data, its exact route binding and generated port projection are fingerprinted on routes and applicable items. Policy presence alone grants no implementation, review or native qualification credit.
- Workflow template and values documents have no literal schema. Their rows come from the closed-key validator calls in workflow_templates.py. Their contract fingerprints the complete normalized module syntax tree, so any code change in that module, including helpers and constants, requires regeneration.
- Shared Studio routes (workflow, workflow-design) inventory only the addresses they own; embedded adaptor documents are counted on their own routes.
- External plugins are owner handoffs. The tracked inventory is built with plugins disabled; uninstalled plugins are not covered and never inherit built-in statuses.
- Generic fallbacks (operation name plus counter) and family rules (folder and file stem) are recorded as such; they do not count as local-context naming for any member until assessed.
