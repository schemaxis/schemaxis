## Boundaries

GetDP is steady one-dimensional scalar conduction; CalculiX covers the declared
linear-elastic rectangular structural member; Gmsh covers the declared primitive/STEP
meshing product; ngspice covers the declared linear AC network. General transient,
nonlinear, coupled and MPI products are not inferred. Oracle's 24-family inventory
retains the unimplemented administration, procedural and advanced-type families.
Its declared effect boundary refuses enabled triggers and undeclared incoming
cascades before mutation; privileged concurrent DDL is outside the contract.

An optional GetDP-to-Calc handoff timed out in the external spreadsheet application;
that optional application combination is not qualified. GetDP's own workflow,
resume, public authoring/reuse and study acceptance passed.

Native receipts qualify named cases on their recorded source and runtimes. Their
conservative source digest includes shared framework and packaged Help. Packaged
receipts bind clean source; combined local working changes are qualified separately
and do not silently become part of those commits. No receipt establishes an
exhaustive application/API ratio or general runtime availability.

No version bump, push, public release or deployment is part of this integration.
