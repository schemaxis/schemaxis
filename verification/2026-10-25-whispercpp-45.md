# whisper.cpp allocation 45: host-only explicit cue deletion (`schemaxis.whispercpp.correct/2`)

Scope: the new separate route `schemaxis.whispercpp.correct/2` (`corrections_deletion.py`) of the whisper.cpp provider, the new closed
output contract `schemaxis.timedtext.reviewed/2` in `schemaxis.core.timed_text`, and the Pandoc `transcript` block's acceptance and
provenance paragraph for a reviewed/2 document. `correct/1`, `reviewed/1`, their plans, publications and genuine receipts, the
transcription route `schemaxis.whispercpp/1`, its official 1.9.5 mapping and its qualification record are unchanged and are not
re-reviewed here. This is a practical product judgement, not customer-demand evidence.

## Why

Real whisper.cpp 1.9.5 with `ggml-base.en` transcribed a two second silent recording as one cue, "You" (retained native probe, draft
SHA-256 `ce623bde...27be`). `correct/1` can only replace cue text with other visible text (blank text is refused) and the shared reader
refuses an empty cue list, so a person could not remove the artefact.

## What the route does

- Same document as `correct/1` plus `deletions`: a distinct array of unique integer **source** indices (`corrections` keeps its exact
  `{index, text}` shape; no delete flag). `DELETION_DUPLICATE`, `CORRECTION_DELETION_CONFLICT` (index replaced and deleted) and
  `INVALID_ARGUMENT` for non-integers are plan-time; `DELETION_INDEX_INVALID` and `CORRECTION_INDEX_INVALID` need the draft and are run-time.
- `basisSha256` pin unchanged (`CORRECTIONS_STALE`). The draft must still be an unedited machine `segments/1`: a reviewed document of either
  version is `DRAFT_NOT_MACHINE_DRAFT`; an original draft with zero cues is `TIMED_TEXT_INVALID` at `$.draft.segments` (reader unchanged for
  `segments/1` and `reviewed/1`).
- `human-corrected` needs at least one replacement or deletion; `human-reviewed-unchanged` needs neither (`CORRECTIONS_STATUS_CONFLICT`).
- Publishes `reviewed-segments.json` (reviewed/2, artifact index 0), `transcript.txt`, `captions.srt`, `captions.vtt`, ledger
  `corrections.json` (`corrections/2`) and optional `caption-review.json`. Retained cues keep their times and are renumbered contiguously from
  zero. The ledger holds `replacements`, `deletions` (source index, times, original text) and `retained` (published index, source index, times,
  original text, final text, edited flag).
- Every cue deleted of a nonempty draft: reviewed/2 holds `segments: []`; `transcript.txt` and `captions.srt` are empty; `captions.vtt` is
  `WEBVTT\n\n`. The shared reader accepts an empty list **only** for reviewed/2 when the deleted and retained source indices partition
  `basis.cueCount` (at least one) exactly, the status is `human-corrected` and nothing is corrected. `captionReview` with nothing retained is
  `CAPTION_REVIEW_EMPTY`.
- Pandoc: a reviewed/2 document is accepted. The paragraph states the deleted source indices and, with no cue left, that no cues are retained
  and no spoken words are published (an author's declaration, not evidence about the recording). With no cue **no table** is published: the
  trusted HTML, DOCX and native-AST readers require a header and a data row, and they are not relaxed. The plan's transcript loss sentence is
  unchanged (planning never opens the file), so existing plans stay byte-identical; the paragraph carries the deletion facts. (Review repair 27 added
  `transcriptObservations` to `report-summary.json`; see the repair section below.)

## Not claimed

- No reviewer identity, no proof audio was heard, no accuracy, readability or accessibility certification, and no claim that a deletion was
  right or that an emptied recording is silent. Every publication and Help text says the status is the author's declaration.
- **No native qualification.** Host-only; no `qualification.json` entry; readiness leaves `nativeQualification` and `runtimeAvailability`
  unknown. The Pandoc runtime stays the already qualified 3.12.1; no native mapping changed, so no upstream version check was needed and
  `upstream.json` is untouched.
- Not covered: timing edits, splits, merges, insertions, importing an edited transcript, a second review round on a reviewed document, drafts
  beyond 512 cues, 256 characters per cue or 32768 in total.
- Retained-input limit (as for `correct/1`): the route has no retained-input hook, so verifying a **workflow** reopens the original
  `draft.path`; verifying the job directory itself replays from `captured-draft.json` and does not need the original. The deleted cues' original text
  and timing are kept in the ledger and in the captured draft, not in the reviewed document.
